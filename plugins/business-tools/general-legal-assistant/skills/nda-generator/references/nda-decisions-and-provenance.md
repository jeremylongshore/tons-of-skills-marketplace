# NDA decisions and provenance

## Four entry points, two distinct questions

Mutual and one-way describe disclosure direction. Employee and vendor describe
relationship context. Establish both rather than assuming each context has one
fixed direction or set of additional covenants. For multiple parties, map each
party's actual disclosure/receipt role and permitted recipients explicitly.

## Fifteen candidate topics

| Topic                  | Decision or evidence                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Parties/purpose        | Actual legal identities, roles and permitted evaluation/use.                                        |
| Protected information  | Categories, designation process and treatment of oral disclosures.                                  |
| Exclusions             | Chosen exclusions and any evidence/conditions needed to apply them.                                 |
| Receiving-party duties | Actual handling/use/disclosure obligations appropriate to the relationship.                         |
| Permitted disclosures  | Representatives, compelled/protected disclosures and relevant legal questions.                      |
| Use restrictions       | Scope of permitted purpose, avoiding an unrelated IP transfer.                                      |
| Term/survival          | Disclosure period versus continuing duties, with explicit start/end choices.                        |
| Return/retention       | Operationally supported deletion/return and retained-copy constraints.                              |
| IP/license/warranty    | Actual requested allocation; do not assume confidentiality transfers ownership.                     |
| Remedies               | Requested terms and applicable review questions; no automatic court-order entitlement.              |
| Separate restrictions  | Non-solicit/non-compete or other restrictions only if separately requested and reviewed in context. |
| Governing law          | Explicit choice and unresolved applicability questions, not an address-derived default.             |
| Disputes               | Requested forum/process; no automatic international arbitration.                                    |
| General/precedence     | Notices, amendments, assignment, existing terms and their interaction.                              |
| Execution fields       | Correct party/representative fields left unsigned, with actual date choices.                        |

Use relevant topics; fifteen headings are not mandatory legal structure or proof
of completeness. Plain-language explanations should be faithful and separate
from operative text unless the user specifically requests an annotated draft.

## Source and template discipline

Record a chosen template's actual URL/file, version, retrieved text, license and
relevant passage. Preserve applicable attribution and label adaptation. A free
template is not automatically suitable for the transaction; a software project's
license does not automatically establish every hosted document's license.

The [Common Paper Mutual NDA page](https://commonpaper.com/standards/mutual-nda/)
and [editing guidance](https://help.commonpaper.com/en/articles/8757714-editing-the-standard-terms)
were read on 2026-10-25. They identify CC BY 4.0 licensing and distinguish standard
terms from changes/adaptations. Verify the actual selected version and preserve
provenance; these instructions do not claim the generated draft was benchmarked,
approved by Common Paper or identical to its standard.

## Jurisdiction-specific questions

Identify the actual legal context before including specialized notices or
restrictions. For US work-related confidentiality, 18 U.S.C. 1833(b) is a relevant
starting point for protected-disclosure and notice questions; verify current
statutory text and applicability before producing a notice. Do not rely only on
an employee heading, assume a generic clause covers every worker, or export a US
rule to other jurisdictions.

[DTSA enactment context](https://www.congress.gov/bill/114th-congress/senate-bill/1890)
is background, not a substitute for current-code verification. If the needed
source cannot be inspected, record the unresolved question rather than claiming
compliance. The draft must not pretend unverified legal work was completed.

## Review record

List the disclosure model, actual/proposed decisions, source versions, changed
clauses, operational dependencies, open questions and draft status. Do not invent
signatures, approval or an effective date to make a draft appear executed. Sending,
signing or publishing an agreement is separate from producing its text.
