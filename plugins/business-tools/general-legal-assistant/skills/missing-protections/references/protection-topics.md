# Protection topics and evidence

## Fifteen candidate topics

These are review prompts, not fifteen mandatory clauses for every agreement.

| Topic                   | Context to inspect                                                                      |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Liability limits        | Which obligations, cap bases and exceptions matter for the represented party?           |
| Indemnification         | Who covers which claims, subject to what process, limits and dependencies?              |
| Convenience termination | Does the transaction need this exit, and what happens to fees/work/data?                |
| Cause termination       | Which breach triggers, cure rights and continuing duties are stated?                    |
| Cure process            | Is a cure mechanism relevant and consistent with termination/remedy provisions?         |
| Notices                 | Which events require notice, by which means, recipient and timing?                      |
| Force majeure           | Are interruption risks relevant, and how do relief, payment and exit interact?          |
| Dispute process         | Which forum/procedure/cost allocation is stated and applicable?                         |
| Governing law           | What provisions identify the intended jurisdiction or require clarification?            |
| Assignment              | What transfers are allowed, restricted or conditioned, and with what exceptions?        |
| Amendments              | How can terms change, including incorporated policies and unilateral rights?            |
| Severability            | What does the text provide about invalid provisions, and which legal questions remain?  |
| Agreement/precedence    | Which documents are incorporated and how do conflicting terms interact?                 |
| Confidentiality         | Is confidential information involved, and which uses/disclosures/limits matter?         |
| Data protection         | Are personal data and processing roles involved, and which verified requirements apply? |

## Type-specific questions

- **Freelance/services:** scope, acceptance, payment, revisions, cancellations,
  expenses and rights in background work/deliverables.
- **Employment:** supplied compensation/benefits, duties, IP, exit and applicable
  employment-law questions; do not assume a generic contractor template fits.
- **NDA:** disclosure direction/purpose, covered information, exclusions, permitted
  recipients, compelled-disclosure process, retention and duration choices.
- **SaaS/software:** license/service scope, actual service commitments, data roles,
  incident handling, export/deletion, price changes and continuity.
- **MSA/SOW:** incorporation, precedence, changes, acceptance, warranties, insurance
  and how the supplied SOW modifies the master terms.
- **Partnership or another type:** identify the transaction and relevant duties
  before adopting prompts from a different type.

Do not call a parameter standard or a provision legally required without support.
Explain what the chosen benchmark contributes and what it cannot establish.

## Status and finding record

Use addressed, partial, conflicting, not found in reviewed material, unreviewed,
or not relevant with a reason. Include the topic, status, source version/locator,
minimal evidence, applicability rationale, possible effect, dependencies, priority
and next question. Keep incomplete coverage separate from actual absence.

Counts require a stated denominator and reconciliation with the register. A
coverage percentage is not a legal safety or compliance certification.

## Correct notification-role distinction

Reviewed 2026-10-25 against the [EDPB Guidelines 9/2022, version 2.0](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-04/edpb_guidelines_202209_personal_data_breach_notification_v2.0_en.pdf),
paragraphs 29 and 43-46, addressing GDPR Article 33. Controller notification to the
supervisory authority is subject to the Article 33(1) conditions and qualified
seventy-two-hour timeframe after awareness. A processor's notice to the controller
under Article 33(2) is without undue delay; it is not a blanket seventy-two-hour
processor deadline. Actual applicability, role, trigger and recipient must be checked.

The [official GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) is the
statutory reference. Do not copy a regulator-notification deadline into a service
contract as if it universally satisfied every recipient's or jurisdiction's duty.
Contract terms may need earlier operational notice; an invented deadline does not
establish compliance. Verify the actual rule for any California or other regime
rather than reusing the CCPA label for every breach-notification requirement.

## Drafting and sources

If drafting is requested, label original proposed text and business assumptions.
If using a template, inspect its actual text, current chosen version, license and
attribution obligations. Do not cite fictional section/version labels or present
an adapted clause as pre-approved for the user's transaction. Check definitions,
precedence, related obligations and commercial choices before suggesting placement.

A gap report alone does not authorize editing contracts, accepting terms,
contacting counterparties or submitting a regulatory notification.
