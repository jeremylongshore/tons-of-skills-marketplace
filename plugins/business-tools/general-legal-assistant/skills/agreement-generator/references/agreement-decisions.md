# Agreement decisions and provenance

## Ten relationship types

| Type          | Establish before drafting                                                                                |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Freelancer    | Actual scope, deliverables/acceptance, payment, IP choices and working-fact questions.                   |
| Partnership   | Purpose, contributions, ownership/economics, authority, governance and exit decisions.                   |
| NDA           | Disclosure direction, permitted purpose, protected information and explicit duration/handling choices.   |
| Licensing     | Identified rights, grant scope, territory, exclusivity, fees and sublicense/use restrictions.            |
| Consulting    | Services/deliverables, responsibilities, fee base, expenses, acceptance and changes.                     |
| SOW           | Master agreement/precedence, deliverables, milestones, dependencies, acceptance and change process.      |
| MSA           | Actual service framework, SOW incorporation, responsibilities and requested risk/operational allocation. |
| Joint venture | Actual form/purpose, contributions, control, economics and wind-down; no entity formation inferred.      |
| Distribution  | Products, territory, channels, exclusivity, ordering, inventory and support choices.                     |
| Referral      | Qualification, commission base, attribution, payment trigger, reporting and reversals/exclusions.        |

The type routes questions; it does not make every confidentiality, payment,
indemnity, IP, non-compete or liability clause mandatory. Select relevant sections
and identify missing decisions. A multiparty deal requires actual role/obligation
mapping, not just adding more signature columns.

## Decision record

For each material choice record the supplied fact or proposed option, source,
party/obligation affected, units/dates, dependencies and unresolved review question.
Reuse known decisions. If the user delegates choosing options, keep them labeled
as proposals until their status actually changes.

Do not invent party names, entity types, notice contacts, effective dates, fees,
benchmarks, remedies, arbitration or rights transfers to make a document look
complete. A notice address does not establish incorporation or governing law.

Check definitions, reciprocal obligations, payment/acceptance, termination/survival,
IP/licenses, data handling, schedules and precedence together. Preserve relevant
requirements rather than optimizing for a round clause count.

## Sources, licensing and benchmarking

Inspect the actual selected template before relying on it. Record its source,
version/text, license and applicable attribution; distinguish direct copying,
adaptation and original proposed wording. A repository/tool license does not
by itself establish the license of every document it distributes.

[Common Paper standard agreements](https://commonpaper.com/standards/) and its
[editing guidance](https://help.commonpaper.com/en/articles/8757714-editing-the-standard-terms)
are source/provenance starting points. Its published materials identify CC BY 4.0
licensing; verify the actual selected asset and preserve attribution. Do not call
modified text the unchanged standard or claim an unperformed benchmark or author
approval. Free availability is not evidence of suitability for the user's deal.

If legal adjustments are requested, identify the current jurisdiction, actual
facts and primary authority used. A short state/country label or industry name
cannot establish which worker-status, consumer, tax or dispute rule applies.
Unsupported assertions remain questions for qualified review.

## Draft and review artifacts

Keep the requested agreement copy separate from assumptions, source notes and
plain-language explanations unless the user asks for an annotated draft. Mark
unresolved material decisions and actual draft status. Do not invent signatures,
party acceptance or execution. Sending, signing, filing or implementing a deal
is a distinct authorized action, not an implicit generation step.
