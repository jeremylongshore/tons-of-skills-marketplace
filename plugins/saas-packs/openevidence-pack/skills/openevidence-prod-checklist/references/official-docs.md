# OpenEvidence Official Evidence Register

Evidence reviewed: 2026-09-11.

## Authority boundary

- Use current first-party OpenEvidence pages and the institution's executed agreements as authority.
- The public user guide documents end-user web and mobile workflows. This audit found no first-party public SDK, API, webhook, service-account, developer-environment, or numeric rate-limit contract.
- Negative discovery is not proof that a private enterprise capability does not exist. Treat it as unsupported until the vendor and contract owner provide current written documentation.
- OpenEvidence's Terms describe the service as educational and informational, not a diagnostic substitute, and keep clinical judgment with qualified professionals.
- PHI, recording, consent, retention, security, and institutional commitments must be resolved from applicable law, policy, BAA, MSA, SLA, and other written agreements.
