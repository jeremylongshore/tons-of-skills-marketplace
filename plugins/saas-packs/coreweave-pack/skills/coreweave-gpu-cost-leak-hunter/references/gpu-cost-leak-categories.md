# GPU usage review categories

Preserve historical anchors CW-C1 through CW-C4 as questions, not proof of waste.

1. **CW-C1: idle reservation exposure.** Correlate utilization with workload phases and contractual obligations. Idle RI capacity remains billed; removing Pods need not release the commitment.
2. **CW-C2: right-sizing scenario.** Compare current rates and a representative measured workload. Model size or a lower hourly rate alone does not establish lower cost per successful token.
3. **CW-C3: idle-looking allocation.** Exclude checkpoints, data-loading, scheduled demand and missing samples before recommending a reversible capacity experiment.
4. **CW-C4: commitment scenario.** Compare RI/Flex/Spot/On-Demand terms, minimums, preemption and demand uncertainty. A discount is an owner-supplied scenario, not a guaranteed saving.

These categories can overlap. Never sum them into recovered cash or an additive savings headline without an independent non-overlap and billing reconciliation.

Sources: [capacity plans](https://docs.coreweave.com/platform/capacity-plans), [usage monitoring](https://docs.coreweave.com/observability/usage-monitoring).
