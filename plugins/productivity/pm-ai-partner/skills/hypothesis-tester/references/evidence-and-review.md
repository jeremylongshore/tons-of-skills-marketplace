# Hypothesis Test Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist before recommending an experiment or interpreting its result.

1. Use `Glob` to locate experiment artifacts, `Grep` to find metric definitions and prior tests,
   and `Read` to inspect the relevant design and analysis files.
2. Express the hypothesis as a falsifiable prediction with population, intervention, outcome,
   comparison, and time window.
3. Define the primary metric, guardrails, minimum detectable effect, assignment unit, duration,
   stopping rule, and analysis population before exposure.
4. Check contamination, novelty effects, sample-ratio mismatch, multiple comparisons, missingness,
   and practical significance—not only a p-value.
5. Report the decision, uncertainty, limitations, and what follow-up evidence would reverse it.

If randomization is not feasible, choose an alternative design explicitly and narrow the causal
claim to what that design supports.
