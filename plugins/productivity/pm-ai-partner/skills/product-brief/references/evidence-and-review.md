# Product Brief Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist before calling a product brief ready for decision or engineering review.

1. Use `Glob` to locate research and specifications, `Grep` to trace prior decisions and metric
   definitions, and `Read` to inspect the authoritative evidence.
2. Name the affected user, observed problem, current workaround, frequency, severity, and evidence.
   Keep the solution out of the problem statement.
3. Define in-scope behavior, explicit exclusions, success and guardrail metrics, dependencies,
   risks, rollout, rollback, and unresolved decisions.
4. Distinguish a confirmed fact from an assumption. Give each high-risk assumption a validation
   method and owner.
5. Review the brief with design and engineering for feasibility, then record accepted changes and
   remaining dissent.

Replace placeholders before handoff. Dates, targets, and estimates must either cite their basis or
be labeled as proposals requiring confirmation.
