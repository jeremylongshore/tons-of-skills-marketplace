# Competitor Analysis Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist to keep competitive analysis decision-grade rather than promotional.

1. Use `Glob` to locate relevant local research, `Grep` to find named competitors and prior
   claims, and `Read` to inspect the underlying evidence.
2. Label every claim as observed product behavior, vendor claim, customer report, or inference.
   Record the source date when recency changes the conclusion.
3. Compare competitors against the same user job and buyer, not an arbitrary feature list.
4. Separate a missing feature from a demonstrated competitive disadvantage. Explain what evidence
   would move an item from Ignore or Monitor to Respond or Lead.
5. End with a bounded recommendation, confidence level, disconfirming evidence, and the next
   review trigger.

Do not present marketing copy as verified behavior, infer adoption from feature availability, or
recommend roadmap work without connecting it to an affected user outcome.
