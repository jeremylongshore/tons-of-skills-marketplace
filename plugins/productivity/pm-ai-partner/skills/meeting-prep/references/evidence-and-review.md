# Meeting Preparation Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist for a decision, escalation, negotiation, or alignment meeting.

1. Use `Glob` to locate prior notes and plans, `Grep` to find decisions and unresolved questions,
   and `Read` to inspect the authoritative context.
2. Write one desired outcome and one explicit ask. If neither exists, state that the meeting is
   informational and define the useful output.
3. Separate known facts, assumptions, stakeholder positions, and open questions.
4. Prepare the opening, three evidence-backed talking points, likely objections, concise answers,
   acceptable tradeoffs, and a fallback outcome.
5. End the brief with owner, decision, next action, and follow-up date fields for live capture.

Do not invent an attendee's position or bury a difficult ask in background. Flag missing context
that materially changes the recommended posture.
