# PM Writing Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering a brief, update, email, memo, or presentation narrative.

1. Use `Glob` to locate relevant source documents, `Grep` to find facts and terminology, and `Read`
   to inspect the authoritative context.
2. State the audience, desired action, governing conclusion, and evidence hierarchy before drafting.
3. Lead with the conclusion or ask. Use only the context required to understand and trust it.
4. Mark uncertain claims, verify figures and dates, preserve source meaning, and remove unsupported
   superlatives or invented quotations.
5. Review for factual accuracy, audience fit, structure, plain language, accessibility, and a clear
   next step. Produce alternate lengths only when they serve distinct channels.

Never expose confidential information merely because it appears in source material. If evidence is
incomplete, use an explicit placeholder with an owner rather than fabricate precision.
