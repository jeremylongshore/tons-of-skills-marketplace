# Strategic Clarity Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist at each phase boundary rather than waiting until the final charter.

1. Use `Glob` to locate charters and plans, `Grep` to find ownership claims and commitments, and
   `Read` to inspect the documents that define current authority.
2. During Absorb, label facts, interpretations, tensions, and missing voices separately.
3. During Audit, compare stated ownership with actual decisions, capabilities, dependencies, and
   outcomes. Record contradictions instead of smoothing them over.
4. During Articulate, offer alternative boundary and value-proposition options with explicit
   tradeoffs. Define what the team will stop doing.
5. During Align, capture objections, decisions, owners, validation signals, and a review date.

Do not treat consensus as proof of clarity. A durable result names decision rights, interfaces,
success measures, exclusions, and the evidence that would justify revising them.
