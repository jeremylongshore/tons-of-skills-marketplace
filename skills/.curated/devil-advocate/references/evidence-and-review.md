# Devil's Advocate Evidence and Review Checklist

Use this checklist to make critique specific, proportionate, and useful.

1. Use `Glob` to find relevant plans, `Grep` to locate assumptions and commitments, and `Read` to
   inspect the evidence that supports the proposal.
2. Restate the proposal's strongest version, intended outcome, and constraints before challenging
   it. Do not attack a weaker substitute.
3. Rank objections by likelihood, impact, reversibility, and evidence quality. Separate fatal
   flaws from mitigable risks and preferences.
4. For each material objection, name a mitigation or a test that could resolve it.
5. Close with the strongest surviving option, residual risks, and the evidence that would change
   the recommendation.

Avoid generic pessimism, invented stakeholder views, and false balance. A useful challenge makes
the decision stronger even when the original proposal survives.
